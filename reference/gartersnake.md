# Maritime Gartersnake Size and Mass Data from the Maritimes

This is a subset of data collected by Issac Acker during his Honours
thesis at Mount Allison University on snakes in Nova Scotia and New
Brunswick. It includes morphometric data for 46 Maritime Gartersnakes.
The paper associated with this data is: Acker I, Baxter-Gilbert J, Riley
JL. 2025. Assessing the presence of Ophidiomyces ophiodiicola on snakes
in New Brunswick and Nova Scotia. Herpetological Conservation & Biology,
20(3): 624-638. URL:
https://www.herpconbio.org/Volume_20/Issue_3/Acker_etal_2025.pdf

## Usage

``` r
gartersnake
```

## Format

### `gartersnake`

A data frame with 46 rows and 6 columns:

- id_num:

  A unique identifier for each individual snake measured.

- age_class:

  Either J for juvenile or A for adult, depending on the age of the
  snake.

- sex:

  Either F for female, M for male, or NA if the snake was not sexually
  mature yet (i.e., a juvenile).

- mass_g:

  Mass, in grams, of the snake.

- svl_mm:

  Snout-vent length (SVL) in mm of the snake. SVL is the distance from
  the snake's snout to the anterior edge of their cloaca.

- total_length_mm:

  Total length in mm of the snake.
